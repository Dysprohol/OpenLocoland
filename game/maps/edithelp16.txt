CITADEL, CHARYBDIS, FLY NESTS, MINES

THE CITADEL (PIRATES) COMES WITH ITS TOWER,
GRATES AND CORKSCREWS AS ONE OBJECT. IN
PLAY ITS GATE KILLS ALL BUT ITS TOWER'S
ARMY WHILE THE TOWER STANDS.
CHARYBDIS (SWAMP) GOES ON THE TRACK WITH
ITS EYE, TEETH AND GUNS; IT PULLS IN OTHER
ARMIES' TRAINS, EATS YOURS. KILL ITS EYE.
FLY NEST (UNITS): ITS PANEL - GREEN FLIES
IN WAVES OR GREY ONES ON GUARD.
MINE (UNITS): ON THE TRACK, IN THE ARMY OF
THE PALETTE'S ROW; ONLY ITS ARMY SEES IT.
