SETS OF THE MISSIONS

CRUSHER (PIRATES): STANDS AND DRUMS ACROSS
A TRACK BITE ALL BUT ITS ARMY EVERY 25 S;
IT STOPS WHEN ITS ARMY'S TOWERS FALL
OR NEVER (ITS PANEL).
SCYLLA (SWAMP): ITS JAWS ACROSS A DIAGONAL
TRACK BITE EVERYONE EVERY 15 S.
THESE COME WHOLE TOO, THEIR PANEL SETS
THE PART: BORDER POST AND ITS CORKSCREW,
LEVEL EXIT AND ITS TOP, FLAGPOLE OR
STATION AND ITS FLAG, CHIMNEYS AND THEIR
SMOKE OR GLOW, OIL DERRICK, POOL. NO
TOWERS LEFT: THE CORKSCREWS BREAK.
