SETS OF THE MISSIONS

CRUSHER (PIRATES): TWO STANDS AND DRUMS
ACROSS A TRACK; EVERY 25 S IT BITES ALL
BUT ITS ARMY. SCYLLA (SWAMP): ITS JAWS
ACROSS A DIAGONAL TRACK BITE EVERYONE
EVERY 15 S. THE TRACK TURNS BOTH.
THESE COME WHOLE TOO, THEIR PANEL SETS
THE PART: BORDER POST AND ITS CORKSCREW,
LEVEL EXIT AND ITS TOP, FLAGPOLE OR
STATION AND ITS FLAG, CHIMNEYS AND THEIR
SMOKE OR GLOW, OIL DERRICK, POOL.
WHEN ALL THE PIRATE TOWERS FALL, THE
CORKSCREWS BREAK.
