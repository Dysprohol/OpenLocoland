PLACING: TRACK, OVERLAP, FLAGS

AN OBJECT GOES WHERE YOU PUT IT, ALSO OVER
OTHER OBJECTS. NEAR A RAIL A FACTORY,
REPAIR POINT, FORT, MINING PLANT, POST,
CRATE OR WRECK SNAPS ONTO THE TRACK;
SHIFT OR NO TRACK NEAR: IT STAYS WHERE
THE CURSOR IS. A MINE GOES ONTO A RAIL
POINT ONLY. NOTHING IN A FACTORY'S DOOR:
THE FACTORY DELETES WHAT STANDS THERE.
A FLAG FLIES ON A POLE OF ITS OWN, OR GOES
ONTO THE POLE OR STATION UNDER THE CURSOR;
SHIFT: THE FLAG ALONE, WHERE YOU WANT.
