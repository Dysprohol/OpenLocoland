JOINTS OF THE TRACK

TRACK TOOL: YELLOW - AN END OF TRACK.
RED - IT LOOKS JOINED BUT IS NOT (A FEW PX
SHORT, TWO HEIGHTS, AN ANGLE), OR TRAINS
CAN ONLY TURN ROUND THERE (A V).
LAYING: THE END GREEN - IT JOINS THE TRACK,
ORANGE - IT STOPS AT A RAIL UNJOINED.
SNAP: ON - AN END A FEW PX OFF IS LAID TO
MEET THE RAIL; OFF FOR TRACKS KEPT APART.
MENU, CHECK THE TRACK: J THE NEXT PLACE,
SHIFT+J THE ONE BEFORE, ESC DONE.
