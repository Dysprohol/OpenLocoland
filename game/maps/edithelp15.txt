TURNING: R, E AND THE WHEEL

R TURNS RIGHT, E LEFT, A STEP OF THE
OBJECT'S DIRECTIONS (A FORT 8, A UNIT 32;
A DECAL'S ARE ITS LOOKS); GUNS GO ALONG.
ONE OF A SINGLE DIRECTION STAYS.
R AND E TURN ROUND: A TRAIN, A WRECK ON
THE TRACK, A SEMAPHORE BESIDE THE TRACK
(IT CROSSES TO THE TRAINS OF THE OTHER
WAY), A PUSH LINE, A REPAIR POINT, A FORT.
A FACTORY AND A BLOCKER FOLLOW THEIR
TRACK: DRAG THEM ALONG IT.
RAILS DO NOT TURN: LAY THEM AGAIN.
BEFORE PLACING, R / E TURN THE PREVIEW.
