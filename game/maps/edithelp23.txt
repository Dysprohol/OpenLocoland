TRACK: THE RAILS' LATTICE

A RAIL IS ONE OF 12 SHAPES - 4 STRAIGHT,
8 CURVES - ON A GRID OF 71 X 50 PX (OR
HALVES): 8 WAYS, NOTHING BETWEEN THEM.
A CLICK LAYS WHAT IT POINTS AT: OVER OTHER
RAILS, ACROSS THEIR JOINTS, A DEAD END, A
SHARP TURN, THE SAME RAIL AGAIN. RAILS
LINK WHERE THEIR ENDS MEET POINTING
OPPOSITE WAYS AT ONE HEIGHT, SO A TURN
NEEDS A CURVE AND A CROSSING IS NO SWITCH.
A POINT TAKES 6 RAILS AT MOST: THE ONLY
RAIL NOT LAID.
