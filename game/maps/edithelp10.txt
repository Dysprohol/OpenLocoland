SAVING AND PLAYING

CTRL+S SAVES THE MAP. A NEW MAP, POLIGON
AND THE TEMPLATE (AN UPDATE WRITES THEM
AGAIN) ASK FOR A NAME OF THEIR OWN, GET A
FOLDER IN MAPS AND ARE ON CUSTOM MAPS.
MENU: NEW MAP, LOAD, SAVE (UNDER A NEW
NAME), EXPORT AND IMPORT (A .ZIP FILE),
RENAME OR DELETE, MISSION SETTINGS, MAP
SIZE, PLAY, UNDO, REDO, CHECK THE TRACK,
QUICK TEST, BACK TO THE MENU, EXIT.
PLAY: THE SAVED MAP AS A MISSION, THEN BACK
HERE. QUICK TEST (CTRL+R): HERE, SAVED OR
NOT; CTRL+R STOPS IT. PLAY ASKS TO SAVE.
