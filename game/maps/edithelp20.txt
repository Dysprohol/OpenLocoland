NEUTRALS AND PIRATES

ON AN EDITOR MAP A NEUTRAL (YELLOW) NEVER
FIRES FIRST. WHEN A SIDE HITS IT, IT AND THE
NEUTRALS THAT SEE IT ANSWER THAT SIDE ONLY,
TO THE END, AND THAT SIDE MAY FIRE AT THEM.
AN ARMED ONE'S PANEL: IT FIRES WHEN
ATTACKED (AS IT STARTS) OR NEVER.
PIRATES (BLACK) FIGHT EVERY SIDE AND EVERY
SIDE FIGHTS THEM. A NEUTRAL FACTORY IS THE
PLAYER'S WHEN HIS TRAIN STOPS AT ITS LIGHT,
A REPAIR POINT WHEN HIS TRAIN ENTERS IT.
THE CAMPAIGN'S MISSIONS KEEP THE GAME'S
OWN RULES.
