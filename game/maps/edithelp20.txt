NEUTRALS AND PIRATES

ON A MAP OF THE EDITOR A NEUTRAL (YELLOW)
NEVER FIRES FIRST. WHEN A SIDE HITS IT,
IT AND THE NEUTRALS THAT SEE IT ANSWER
THAT SIDE ONLY, TO THE END OF THE MISSION,
AND THAT SIDE MAY FIRE AT THEM THEN.
AN ARMED ONE'S PANEL: IT FIRES WHEN
ATTACKED (AS IT STARTS) OR NEVER.
PIRATES (BLACK) FIGHT EVERY SIDE AND
EVERY SIDE FIGHTS THEM.
THE CAMPAIGN'S MISSIONS KEEP THE GAME'S
OWN RULES.
