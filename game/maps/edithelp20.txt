NEUTRALS AND PIRATES

ON AN EDITOR MAP A NEUTRAL (YELLOW) NEVER
FIRES FIRST. WHEN A SIDE HITS IT, IT AND THE
NEUTRALS THAT SEE IT ANSWER THAT SIDE ONLY,
TO THE END, AND THAT SIDE MAY FIRE AT THEM.
AN ARMED ONE'S PANEL: IT FIRES WHEN ATTACKED
(AS IT STARTS) OR NEVER.
PIRATES (BLACK) FIGHT EVERY SIDE AND EVERY
SIDE FIGHTS THEM. A NEUTRAL FACTORY IS
TAKEN WHEN THE PLAYER'S TRAIN COMES TO ITS
LIGHT, A REPAIR POINT TO ITS LINE'S BEGIN
(SAVE AN OLDER MAP AGAIN). THE CAMPAIGN'S
MISSIONS KEEP THE GAME'S OWN RULES.
