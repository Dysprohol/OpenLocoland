THE MISSION

PUSH LINE: CLICK ITS BEGIN AND ITS END
ON THE TRACK. T PUSHES OR NOT, R TURNS
IT ROUND, S PUTS SEMAPHORES, DEL
DELETES IT. MARK THE FACTORY'S EXIT,
THE REPAIR POINT AND THE MAP'S ENTRY
AND EXIT THIS WAY.
PUT THE REINFORCEMENT POINT ON THE
MAIN LINE PAST THE EDGE OF THE MAP.
MISSION SETTINGS: TITLE, MONEY, TIME
OF DAY, ENEMY TRAINS, TEXTS, BRIEFING.
A SAVED MAP IS LISTED ON CUSTOM MAPS.
