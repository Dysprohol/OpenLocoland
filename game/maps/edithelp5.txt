LAYING TRACK

CLICK A RAIL'S END OR A START POINT, THEN
WHERE THE SECTION GOES: ONE RAIL NEAR IT,
A RUN WITH CURVES FARTHER; THERE IT ENDS.
RMB OR ESC CANCELS THE START, A WHEEL
CLICK DELETES A RAIL.
- A TURN GOES THROUGH A CURVE
- A BRANCH: A STRAIGHT RAIL AND A CURVE
  WITH THE SAME MIDDLE (A Y JUNCTION)
GREEN ENDS: IT IS LAID. RED: THAT POINT
HAS 6 RAILS. V: PLAIN, SWAMP OR DESTROYED
RAILS; WHEEL, PGUP / PGDN: THE NEXT RAIL
CLIMBS OR FALLS.
