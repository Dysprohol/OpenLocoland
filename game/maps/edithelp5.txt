THE MISSION

PUSH LINE: CLICK ITS BEGIN AND ITS END ON
THE TRACK. A BAR MARKS ITS BEGIN, ARROWS
ITS WAY. SELECT A LINE BY ITS BAR OR
ARROW (SELECT TOOL TOO): DEL DELETES IT,
T PUSHES OR NOT, R TURNS IT ROUND, RMB
OPENS ITS PANEL. A SEMAPHORE BRINGS ITS
LINE AND TAKES IT ALONG WHEN DELETED.
MARK THE MAP'S ENTRY AND EXIT THIS WAY.
TRAIN ARRIVALS (0): A TRAIN COMES IN.
MISSION SETTINGS: TITLE, MONEY, TIME
OF DAY, ENEMY TRAINS, TEXTS, BRIEFING.
A SAVED MAP IS LISTED ON CUSTOM MAPS.
