THE GRAVEL BED

THE BED IS AN OBJECT OF THE GAME UNDER EACH
RAIL. NEW TRACK GETS THE ONE CHOSEN IN THE
TRACK ROW (MEADOW, ICE, VOLCANIC, DESERT,
NONE; B CYCLES). PAINT > GRAVEL BED > LAYS:
OBJECTS SWAPS IT UNDER TRACK ALREADY LAID:
PICK A BED PICTURE, DRAG ALONG THE RAILS -
THE RAILS IN THE BRUSH GET IT, RMB / SHIFT
TAKES IT AWAY, UNDER THE WHOLE TRACK: ALL.
LAYS: BAND PAINTS A BAND INTO THE GROUND'S
PICTURE INSTEAD (KIND, WIDTH; RMB PUTS THE
ORIGINAL BACK). THE DECORATIONS BEDS DO THE
OBJECT SWAP TOO. ONE STROKE IS ONE UNDO.
